How we work

Ranges and reasons, not optimistic promises

Tolerance, lead time and finish all depend on geometry, material and quantity. We give a planning figure, explain what moves it, and confirm the specifics against the drawing before the job starts - because a remade part costs far more than a realistic lead time.

FAQ

Common questions

What do you need to quote a part?

A 3D model (STEP, IGES or native CAD), a drawing with tolerances and datums, the material, the quantity and the finish. Quantity matters because setup cost is spread across it.

Can you help if the design is not finalised?

Yes. Sending a concept early usually saves money, because the manufacturability feedback can change the design while changes are still cheap.

Do you do prototypes and production?

Yes. Prototypes and small batches run on the same processes as production, so a design that works as a prototype will still work at volume.

Can you work from a sample part with no drawing?

Often yes - the part can be measured and a model built, though a drawing is always better because it states which dimensions are actually critical.
