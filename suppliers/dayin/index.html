Overview

DAYIN is a plastic injection molding manufacturer that reports more than 30 years of operation, a fleet of over 100 machines and plants in both China and Vietnam. It offers OEM/ODM, mold design and assembly services.

What they make

  • Plastic injection molding
  • Mold / tooling design
  • OEM/ODM parts and sub-assembly

Who typically buys

  • Brands needing molded plastic parts
  • Buyers diversifying production across China and Vietnam
  • OEM projects requiring mold design through assembly

Sourcing notes

  • The '100+ machines' and China/Vietnam footprint are the company's own claims; confirm the machine tonnage range relevant to your part.
  • Share 3D files, material grade, annual volume and finishing requirements for an accurate tooling and unit-cost quote.
