Overview

Yiwu Jiteng Toys manufactures custom toys and describes itself as a Chinese toy factory with more than 25 years of experience. Its line includes custom squishy toys, slime kits, air-dry clay and educational toys, offered for OEM and wholesale orders.

What they make

  • Custom squishy / novelty toys
  • Slime kits and craft play sets
  • Air-dry clay and educational toys

Who typically buys

  • Toy wholesalers and importers
  • Retail and e-commerce brands needing private label
  • Educational-product buyers

Sourcing notes

  • Safety compliance matters most in toys: request age grading and current test reports for your destination market before ordering.
  • The '25+ years' and factory claims are the company's own; verify the production scope for your specific item.
