All Suppliers

Profiles of the manufacturers and suppliers covered in this guide.

Motorcycles & Electric Vehicles

Zukida Motorcycle

Zukida Motorcycle designs and supplies motorcycles and electric two-wheelers aimed at buyers who want dependable performance at a competitive price. The company positions itself around performance engineering and worldwide distribution.

Shower & Bathroom Hardware

Zimmor Hardware

Zimmor Hardware manufactures frameless shower door hardware and describes itself as a QCC-certified factory operating since 1999. Its range covers shower hinges, glass clamps and custom fittings for wholesale and OEM buyers.

Dropshipping Fulfilment & Sourcing

Dropioneer

Dropioneer provides dropshipping fulfilment together with product sourcing, custom packaging and international shipping for Shopify and DTC brands. It advertises a zero-upfront-inventory model, handling packing and dispatch on the seller's behalf.

Toys & Educational Products

Jiteng Toys (Yiwu Jiteng Toys)

Yiwu Jiteng Toys manufactures custom toys and describes itself as a Chinese toy factory with more than 25 years of experience. Its line includes custom squishy toys, slime kits, air-dry clay and educational toys, offered for OEM and wholesale orders.

Cables & Electronic Connectors

Longkai Electronics

Longkai Electronics manufactures custom USB, Mini SAS and industrial cables, supplying certified high-performance connectors for industrial and computing applications. The company offers OEM and ODM cable design and production.

Logistics & Sorting Automation

TrueLiSort

TrueLiSort designs custom parcel sorting systems for logistics and e-commerce operations, covering cross-belt, narrow-belt and wheel sorters along with put walls, DWS scanning and WCS software integration.

Laser Welding & Cutting Equipment

TrueSyn Robotic

TrueSyn Robotic manufactures advanced laser equipment, including laser welding robots, laser cleaning machines and robotic laser cutting systems for industrial production lines.

Plastic Injection Molding

DAYIN

DAYIN is a plastic injection molding manufacturer that reports more than 30 years of operation, a fleet of over 100 machines and plants in both China and Vietnam. It offers OEM/ODM, mold design and assembly services.
