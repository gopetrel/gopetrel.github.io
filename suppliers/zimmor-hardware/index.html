Overview

Zimmor Hardware manufactures frameless shower door hardware and describes itself as a QCC-certified factory operating since 1999. Its range covers shower hinges, glass clamps and custom fittings for wholesale and OEM buyers.

What they make

  • Frameless shower hinges
  • Glass clamps and brackets
  • Custom bathroom hardware fittings

Who typically buys

  • Bathroom-fittings wholesalers
  • Glass and shower-enclosure fabricators
  • OEM brands needing private-label hardware

Sourcing notes

  • The QCC certification and 'since 1999' claim are the company's own statements; request the current certificate and scope before relying on them.
  • For OEM work, confirm finishes, tolerances, glass thickness ranges and packaging requirements.
