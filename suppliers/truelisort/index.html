Overview

TrueLiSort designs custom parcel sorting systems for logistics and e-commerce operations, covering cross-belt, narrow-belt and wheel sorters along with put walls, DWS scanning and WCS software integration.

What they make

  • Cross-belt / narrow-belt / wheel sorters
  • Put-wall and DWS systems
  • WCS software integration

Who typically buys

  • Courier and 3PL operations
  • E-commerce fulfilment centres
  • Warehouses upgrading manual sorting

Sourcing notes

  • Sorting projects are engineered per site: throughput, parcel mix and floor space drive the design, so share your actual volumes.
  • Clarify what is supplied as turnkey (mechanical, controls, software, commissioning) versus buyer scope.
