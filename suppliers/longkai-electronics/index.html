Overview

Longkai Electronics manufactures custom USB, Mini SAS and industrial cables, supplying certified high-performance connectors for industrial and computing applications. The company offers OEM and ODM cable design and production.

Who typically buys

  • Electronics OEMs needing custom cable runs
  • Data-storage and server integrators
  • Industrial equipment makers

Sourcing notes

  • For cable sourcing, specify conductor gauge, shielding, connector type, length tolerance and compliance requirements up front.
  • Ask which certifications (e.g. USB-IF, RoHS) apply to the specific part you need.
