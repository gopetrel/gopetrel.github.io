Capabilities

Six processes, run as one route to a finished part

Most parts need more than one operation. Keeping machining, fabrication, finishing and inspection under one roof removes the handoffs where accuracy and accountability are usually lost.

CNC milling

3-, 4- and 5-axis milling in aluminium, stainless, steel, brass and engineering plastics, quoted from your CAD file with the tolerances you actually need.

CNC turning

Turning and turn-mill in shafts, bushings, fittings, connectors and threaded parts, including small-diameter work.

5-axis machining

Simultaneous and indexed 5-axis machining for complex geometry, compound angles and parts that cannot be reached in three axes.

Learn more

Wire & sinker EDM

Quality

Measured, recorded, repeatable

A part is only as good as the evidence that it is right. Dimensions are verified against the model, critical features are measured on a CMM, and an inspection report can travel with the shipment.

For a new part, a first article inspection confirms the process before volume starts - the cheapest possible moment to find a problem.

  • First article inspection on new parts
  • CMM and optical measurement for critical features
  • Material certificates traceable to the mill
  • Inspection reports available with every lot
