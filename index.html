Quality

Measured, recorded, repeatable

A part is only as good as the evidence that it is right. Dimensions are verified against the model, critical features are measured on a CMM, and an inspection report can travel with the shipment.

For a new part, a first article inspection confirms the process before volume starts - the cheapest possible moment to find a problem.

  • First article inspection on new parts
  • CMM and optical measurement for critical features
  • Material certificates traceable to the mill
  • Inspection reports available with every lot
