Process choice

There is usually more than one way to make a part, and the cheapest route changes with volume, geometry and material. These articles compare routes on the things that actually move the price rather than on capability lists. 2 articles.

What this topic covers

  • Process comparison - Machining against moulding, milling against EDM, welding against mechanical fastening - compared on the variables that actually move cost.
  • Volume crossover - Why the cheapest route changes with quantity, and how to find the point where it flips for a specific part.
  • Route selection - Choosing a process from the feature rather than from habit, including when a part should be split across two operations.
  • Downstream effects - How the process choice fixes the tolerance class, the finish options and the inspection plan before a drawing is released.
