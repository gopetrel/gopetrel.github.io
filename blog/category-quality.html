Inspection is only useful if it measures the features the design cares about. These articles cover first article inspection, CMM reports, material certificates and what a useful inspection plan looks like. 4 articles.

What this topic covers

  • Inspection planning - Agreeing which dimensions are functional before the first cut, so inspection measures what the design cares about.
  • First article inspection - What FAI verifies, what belongs in the report, and when it should be repeated rather than assumed.
  • Documentation - Material certificates, heat-treatment records and how far traceability reaches back through a subcontractor.
  • Non-conformance - What a measured value below tolerance means for a delivery decision, and how to avoid repeating it in the next batch.
