Automotive and EV

Volume consistency is the requirement, not the tightest single tolerance. Busbars need conductivity and flatness; brackets need repeatability across a batch and a finish that survives the underbody environment.

  • Busbars, terminals and current-carrying parts
  • Structural brackets and mounting plates
  • Housings for sensors, controllers and battery hardware

Medical and laboratory

Documentation carries as much weight as dimension. Material grade, finish and traceability have to be recorded, and the surface often has to be cleanable and corrosion resistant.

  • Instrument bodies and frames
  • Fluid-handling manifolds and fittings
  • Precision components inside laboratory equipment

Robotics and automation

The tolerance problem is usually at the assembly, not the part: two components must locate together, which makes positional relationships between features more important than individual dimensions.

  • End-effectors, grippers and tooling plates
  • Precision linkages and pivot components
  • Frames, brackets and mounting interfaces

Industrial machinery

Replacement and spare parts are often quoted from a sample or a worn original. Measuring the part and rebuilding a model is normally the first step, and the drawing is agreed from that.

  • Wear components and replacement spares
  • Machine parts rebuilt from a measured sample
  • Tooling, dies and production fixtures

Energy and marine

The environment decides the material. Salt exposure, temperature and pressure drive grade selection, and the finish is chosen for corrosion life rather than appearance.

  • Corrosion-resistant fittings and housings
  • Components for outdoor and offshore equipment
  • Valve and pump parts in demanding service

Aerospace supply chain

Inspection records and material traceability are part of the deliverable, and the drawing revision has to be controlled tightly because an unapproved change is not an option.

  • Structural brackets and fittings
  • Ground support and production tooling
  • Precision parts with full inspection documentation
